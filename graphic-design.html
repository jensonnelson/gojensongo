INDUSTRY: A GRAPHIC SYSTEM FOR THE FLOOR

Graphics and allover print capsule · Self-initiated concept

I serve and bartend alongside people who all wear the same shoe, and they were missing apparel to go with it. INDUSTRY is a graphic system drawn from what is already in the building: paperwork, type, floor paths and glass rings. It runs from a wordmark to four prints to a jersey.

Insight

A room of hospitality workers already chose the shoe. The apparel was missing.

Concept

Four prints drawn from tickets, floor paths and bar rings, with a wordmark and a set of graphic hits.

System

One print engine, six colors and three colorways per print, built to recolor each season.

Product

Tees, aprons, headwear and a jersey, each placed and scaled per garment.

THE JERSEY

  • Why 86: kitchen shorthand for out of stock, and the number on the jersey
  • Body: Guest Check print on a Bleach ground, scaled up so the ticket details read across the chest
  • Side panels: Behind print on Service Black
  • Numerals and wordmark: Oven 86 with a black outline, and an arched INDUSTRY wordmark

THE SYSTEM

  • Season two: Ticket Rail carries over, Guest Check recolors, Table Rings is rebuilt
  • Custom programs: Ticket Rail is built from a text file, so a team can have its own language
  • Graphic hits: wordmark, 86, ALL DAY and HEARD for chests, hems and headwear

JORDAN HOLIDAY KNIT

Holiday jacquard sweater · Self-initiated concept

A holiday sweater that keeps the Jumpman and the Gym Red at the center. The motif, the yarn colors and the pattern bands were designed together, then documented so a vendor can build it.

Insight

Holiday knitwear usually feels like a costume. This one should look like Jordan.

Concept

A Jumpman and tree motif in matched bands, with Gym Red stripes and rib trim.

System

Three yarn colors, six pattern bands, and a repeat sized for a 12-gauge jacquard.

Product

A full tech pack with flats, trims and graded specs from S to 3XL.

FOOTWEAR AND COLOR

Blacktop Air Force 1 · Nike Women's Fall · Self-initiated concepts

Two concepts that start from a place and a season. Blacktop looks at the Air Force 1 as it is lived in on an outdoor court. Women's Fall builds a color story and carries it from apparel to footwear.

WOMEN'S FALL

  • Research: cultural research and moodboards to set the direction
  • Palette: black, slate, peach, blush, warm stone and taupe, with a cool seafoam accent
  • Carry-through: applied across apparel graphics and footwear colorways

BLACKTOP AF1

  • Territory: the Air Force 1 as it is lived in on the outdoor court
  • Materials: tumbled leather, court suede and a sun-bleached line
  • Cost thinking: detail where the eye lands first and standard material elsewhere

NOSTOS
