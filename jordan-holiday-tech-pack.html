01 · Style Summary

The brief, in one garment

A relaxed crew neck pullover carrying an all-over holiday jacquard: Jumpman silhouettes, Christmas trees, and Nordic snowflakes arranged Fair Isle-style across horizontal bands. Deep black ground, white pattern, Gym Red 2x2 rib at neck, cuffs, and hem. Premium seasonal lifestyle, not a novelty sweater that apologizes for itself.

Every motif is true jacquard, knit in, not printed on. The floats are caught, the bands match at the seams, and the red stripe is knit in. That is the whole point.

03 · Fabric & Materials

What it is made of

Body · Jacquard Knit
Nike Sportswear Jacquard Knit: 70% cotton / 25% recycled polyester / 5% elastane, 320 GSM, 12-gauge fully fashioned jacquard, brushed interior, enzyme-washed for vintage hand feel.
Rib Trim · Neck, Cuffs, Hem
Nike Sportswear Rib: 95% cotton / 5% elastane, 380 GSM, 2x2 rib knit. 3 in width at hem, 2.5 in at cuffs, 1 in at neckline. Recovery stretch. Gym Red.
Accent Stripes
Knit-in stripe bands, same yarn composition as body, Gym Red colorway, 0.25 in width.

All yarns meet Nike sustainability standards: BCI or organic cotton, GRS-certified recycled polyester, OEKO-TEX Standard 100. Colorfastness minimum Grade 4 wash, Grade 4 dry rub, Grade 3 to 4 wet rub per ISO 105.

07 · Measurement Spec

Graded, S through 3XL

Click a size to highlight its column. Sample size: L.

Tolerance
+/-0.5 in on all linear measurements; +/-5% on rib stretch. All measurements flat, relaxed, post-wash. Knit may vary +/-3% from yarn tension.

How to measure
Chest: across front, 1 in below armhole, doubled. Body length: HPS to bottom of rib hem. Sleeve: CB neck seam to cuff edge. Hem width and cuff opening: relaxed, doubled. Armhole depth: shoulder seam straight down to underarm.
